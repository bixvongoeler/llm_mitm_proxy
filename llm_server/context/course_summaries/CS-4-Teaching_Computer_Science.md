# CS 4: Teaching Computer Science

## Course Overview

This course prepares undergraduates to serve effectively as teaching assistants in the Computer Science Department. Through learner-centered instruction, students develop pedagogical techniques, discuss ethical and social concerns faced by TAs, and collaboratively problem-solve challenges that arise during teaching.

## Key Topics

- Pedagogical techniques matched to learner needs
- Ethical and social concerns in teaching
- Collaborative problem-solving for TA situations
- Reflective practice in teaching
- Effective communication and support strategies

## Assignments and Projects

- **Short readings**: Regular reading assignments on teaching topics
- **Reflective blog**: Ongoing documentation of learning experiences as a teacher
- **Final presentation**: Short presentation on a topic of interest to deepen understanding of teaching in your TA class

## Course Structure

- **Format**: Learner-centered, discussion-based sessions with active participation required
- **Grading**: Pass/Fail, based primarily on attendance and participation
- **Co-facilitation**: Computer Science faculty participate in some sessions
- **Location**: Olin Center 012

## Prerequisites

- Must be a new undergraduate teaching assistant for CS 10, CS 11, CS 15, or CS 40
- Course is designed for any CS department TA but required for specific courses listed above
