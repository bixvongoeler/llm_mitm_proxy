# CS 160- Algorithms

## Course Overview
Introduction to the design and analysis of algorithms, covering fundamental strategies including divide-and-conquer, greedy methods, and dynamic programming, along with graph algorithms, sorting, searching, integer arithmetic, hashing, and NP-complete problems.

## Key Topics
- Insertion sort and asymptotic complexity (big-O notation)
- Divide-and-conquer strategies
- Greedy algorithms
- Dynamic programming
- Graph algorithms
- Sorting and searching
- Integer arithmetic
- Hashing
- NP-complete problems

## Prerequisites
- **CS 15** (Data Structures)
- **CS/MATH 61** or **MATH 65** (Discrete Mathematics)
- Familiarity with logarithms, summation notation, and geometric series

## Textbook
**Required:** *Introduction to Algorithms* (4th edition) by Cormen, Leiserson, Rivest, and Stein (CLRS) - MIT Press, 2022

**Supplementary:** Kleinberg & Tardos *Algorithm Design*, Dasgupta et al. *Algorithms*, Skiena *The Algorithm Design Manual*
