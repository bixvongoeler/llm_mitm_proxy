# CS 12- Cyber for Future Policymakers

## Course Overview

This course explores the relevance of computer technologies to policy development, providing future policymakers with essential technical knowledge to understand and shape technology policy. Students learn fundamental concepts in computer science and cybersecurity without requiring a technical background.

## Key Topics

- **Internet Architecture and Networking**: How the internet works, basic networking concepts, and infrastructure fundamentals
- **The Web**: Web technologies and their implications for policy
- **Cloud Architectures**: Understanding cloud computing and its policy implications
- **Cryptography**: Fundamentals of encryption and secure communication
- **Security and Privacy**: Cybersecurity threats, data protection, and privacy considerations
- **AI and Machine Learning**: Introduction to artificial intelligence concepts and their societal impact
- **Open-Source Systems**: Understanding open-source software and its role in technology policy
- **Emerging Technologies**: 
  - Quantum computing
  - Post-quantum cryptography

## Prerequisites

No specific technical prerequisites listed. This course is designed for students interested in policy development who want to understand the technical foundations of modern computing and cybersecurity.
