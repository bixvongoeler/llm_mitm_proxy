# CS 131 - Artificial Intelligence

## Course Overview

This course covers the history, theory, and computational methods of artificial intelligence. Students learn fundamental AI concepts including knowledge representation and computational reasoning methods, with exploration of one or two application areas.

## Key Topics

- **Foundations**: History and theoretical underpinnings of AI
- **Knowledge Representation**: Methods for encoding information in computational systems
- **Computational Reasoning**: Algorithms and techniques for automated inference and decision-making
- **Application Areas** (selected topics may vary by semester):
  - Expert systems
  - Robotics
  - Computer vision
  - Natural language understanding
  - Planning and automated reasoning

## Prerequisites

Not explicitly stated in available materials. Students should verify current prerequisites with the department, but AI courses typically require:

## Additional Information

This is a foundational AI course that provides breadth across multiple AI subfields while allowing depth in selected application domains. The course balances theoretical foundations with practical computational methods.
