# CS 150-AEP Artificial Intelligence: Algorithms, Ethics, and Policy

## Course Overview

This course examines the ethics of AI usage across various settings and explores how policy can affect AI adoption. Students will gain combined perspectives on both policy implications and technical aspects of AI algorithms, preparing them to become informed policy experts and decision-makers.

## Key Topics

Based on the official description, the course covers:

- Technical aspects of AI algorithms
- Ethics of AI usage in various societal settings
- Policy frameworks affecting AI adoption and deployment
- Societal impact of AI as a transformative technology
- Decision-making and leadership on AI-related issues
