# CS 150-QCS Quantum Computer Science

## Course Overview
An elementary-level introduction to quantum computing through the computer science lens, presenting quantum mechanics in a language accessible to students from computer science, physics, electrical engineering, mathematics, and chemistry. The course focuses on theoretical foundations rather than physical implementations.

## Key Topics
- **Mathematical Foundations**: Hilbert spaces, Dirac notation, Schrödinger equation
- **Quantum Mechanics Formalism**: Quantum states, unitary operations, Born rule, interference, no-cloning theorem, uncertainty principle, entanglement
- **Digital & Reversible Computation**: Boolean circuits, reversible circuits, matrix representation
- **Quantum Circuits & Protocols**: Single/multi-qubit gates, quantum teleportation, super-dense coding, Hadamard test, swap test
- **Black Box Algorithms**: Deutsch-Jozsa, Bernstein-Vazirani, Simon's algorithm
- **Quantum Simulation**: Hamiltonians, observables, Hamiltonian simulation
- **Shor's Algorithm**: Factoring, quantum Fourier transform, phase estimation
- **Other Algorithms**: Energy estimation, Grover's search, quantum algorithm for linear systems
- **Quantum Error Correction**: Shor's 9-qubit code, stabilizer formalism
