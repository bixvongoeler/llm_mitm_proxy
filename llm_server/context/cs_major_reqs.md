# Tufts University Computer Science Major Requirements

_Reference guide for advising Tufts CS students (Class of 2025 and beyond)_

## Computer Science Electives

Students must complete **3 CS electives** numbered above COMP 15.

## Foundation & Distribution Requirements (Liberal Arts Students)

Liberal Arts CS majors must also satisfy university-wide requirements:

- Writing
- Foreign Language (equivalent to 3 semesters of college-level courses)
- Culture Option (alternative to second language requirement)
- World Civilizations
- Humanities
- Arts
- Social Sciences
- Natural Sciences
- Mathematical Sciences

## Important Exclusions

The following courses **do not count** toward the CS major:

- COMP 10, COMP 11 (introductory courses)
- Any introductory CS course numbered below 15
- MATH 4, 14, 30, 32
- COMP 55, 99, 155
- COMP 182–188
- More than one Directed Study (COMP 193 or 194)

## Key Policies

**Substitutions:** Rarely allowed. Students must obtain departmental approval if needed.

**Double-Counting (Multiple Majors):** Students with more than one major should consult the Bulletin for rules on sharing courses between majors.

**Minors:** No more than two courses used toward a minor may also count toward foundation, distribution, major, or other minor requirements.
